The Maison approach

We design rooms, not racks.

Maison Cellars is a South Florida design studio focused on wine environments that feel fully integrated with their architecture. We work with homeowners, designers, builders and hospitality teams from early concept through installation.

Our work balances two equal responsibilities: creating a memorable experience and protecting the collection. Material, glass, illumination, bottle orientation and climate are resolved together—not as separate decisions.
